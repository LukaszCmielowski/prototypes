Stop guessing. Let the product try settings for you.

Building an AI agent today means trying models and options by hand in chat. The new idea: press one button, give a few example questions, and get a ranked list of what works best.

In one sentence Describe what the agent should be good at → we try many setting combinations → you pick the winner from a simple ranking → save it as your agent.

Today

  • Pick a model yourself
  • Nudge temperature & tools
  • Chat and hope it’s good
  • Save when it “feels right”
→

With Optimize

  • Describe what “good” looks like
  • We try many setting combos
  • You get a clear ranking
  • Save the winner as your agent
